THE BUG (two halves; the first is the owner's B1, the second was found
by the new pin during this landing)

1. Scientific-repr band destroyed. render_f derived its digits from
   javafmt::double_to_string (the shortest round-trip repr) and split
   on '.', but the JVM's repr goes scientific below 1e-3 and at/above
   1e7 — the "E" tail was treated as fraction digits:
     6.149717374e-4  -> j2r "6.149717374"   JVM "0.000614972"
     1.0e-10         -> j2r "1.0E-100000"   JVM "0.000000000"
   Every %f/%.Nf/%,f of such a magnitude was silently wrong. This is
   the bug that made the owner's ported pendulum workload print joint
   angles as 6.542073097 where the JVM printed -0.000654208.

2. Rounding-carry re-split (latent, no fixture pinned it). After a
   HALF_UP bump that carries past the cut ("999" kept to 2 digits is
   "100"), the decimal point moves right by one; the old fixed
   split_at(int_len) printed an extra fraction digit (9.99 at %.1f
   -> "1.00" instead of "10.0") or truncated the integer part at
   precision 0 (9.99 at %.0f -> "1" instead of "10").

THE FIX (crates/j2r-runtime/src/formatter.rs::render_f, ~35 lines)

Normalize the shortest repr to a plain digit string + a decimal-point
position BEFORE the precision rounding — the same parse render_e
already performs:
  repr "6.149717374E-4" -> digits "6149717374", point = -3
  point < 0 shifts leading zeros into the digit string (point -> 0)
  round_idx = point + precision; round_digits HALF_UP
  a carry that grows the result PAST round_idx shifts the point right
    by the overflow (point += grown.len() - round_idx)
  pad to point+precision, split at the (possibly shifted) point
First implementation checked growth against the ORIGINAL digit length
("999" vs "100" are both length 3) — the new pin render_f(9.99, 1)
caught it ("1.00"); the check is against round_idx (the kept-prefix
length). The pin-first law did its job on its own author.
